About the Portal

A helpful guide to campus life

Our purpose

Campus Portal gives students a clear, welcoming place to access announcements, class schedules, and campus resources. It is designed to keep everyday college information easy to find.

Who it is for

The portal is intended for students, prospective students, instructors, and campus staff who need a reliable overview of student-focused activities and services.

Accessibility

The interface uses semantic HTML, descriptive image alternative text, keyboard-friendly links, and clear visible focus indicators. The responsive layout also adapts to smaller screens so content remains readable and usable.
